- **Xbox: PC play only.** The Xbox import now brings in only games that run on PC. Xbox 360 and console-only titles from your Xbox play history (old short names like "PGR 4" or "TC's GRAW", Kinect apps, no playtime) are left out, and the ones imported before are removed at the next sync.
