The first shareable GameShelf: download, extract, double-click **Start GameShelf**.

- Includes its own Python: nothing to install.
- Opens in its own window, with a tray icon by the clock (Open / Quit). It stops by itself about a minute after you close the window, once any sync has finished.
- First-run setup walks you through IGDB and your stores one at a time, and offers desktop and Start menu shortcuts.
- Updates itself: a bar at the top offers **Update now** when a new version is out. Your data folder is never touched.
- Links to stores and sign-in pages open in your normal browser.
