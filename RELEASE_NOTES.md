- **Better VR detection.** For games on Steam, Sunbeam now uses Steam's own "VR Only" label (games with an optional VR mode, like No Man's Sky, stay regular games). For the rest, Apple's headset (visionOS) and a few others now count as VR platforms. This catches Job Simulator, Vacation Simulator, Gorn, Eagle Flight, Star Trek: Bridge Crew and similar. The check runs by itself shortly after Sunbeam starts.
