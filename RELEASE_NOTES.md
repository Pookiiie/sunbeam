Test build 4 of Sunbeam 1.3 (Beta).

- **Your shelves in Curator.** A new section at the bottom shows each shelf as a card with its covers; click one to see that shelf in the Library. Prefer a row of covers per shelf? Settings › Display › *Your shelves in Curator*: Cards or Rows.
- **Arrange Curator.** **Customize** (top right of Curator) lists its sections: drag them or use the arrows to reorder, and switch off any you don't need. **Done** goes back.
