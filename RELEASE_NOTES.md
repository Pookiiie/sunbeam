**Sunbeam 1.1: prices on both stores**

- **Wishlist prices on Steam and PlayStation:** for every wishlist game, Sunbeam now also checks the store whose wishlist doesn't have it (unless you already own it there) and shows the **lowest** price. Its sale badge and end date come along when that lowest price is a sale.
- **Cards** show that lowest price with the store's mark in front: the heart when it's on that store's wishlist, the plain Steam or PlayStation logo when it isn't.
- **Game pages** show one box per store, side by side, the cheapest outlined, each with its sale or regular price and a link to the store page. "Not on the PlayStation Store" (or Steam) when it isn't sold there.
- "On sale" (the filter, the Curator row and the cover badge) now means the lowest price is a sale price.
- **Platform tags** on game pages show each store's icon next to its name.
