- **Ubisoft Connect refreshes by itself.** Sign in once (Settings › Ubisoft Connect: sign in at connect.ubisoft.com with **Remember me** ticked, then click the Send to Sunbeam bookmark there); after that every sync pulls your Ubisoft games, hours and last played on its own. The bookmark now finds the "stay signed in" pass on Ubisoft's Connect web app too, which it missed before.
- Settings › Ubisoft Connect shows the state plainly: signed in and refreshing by itself (with the last refresh time), or what to do if not. If a refresh fails, the Sync log says so and keeps the last list, instead of reporting "games imported" as if nothing was wrong.
