Test build 2 of Sunbeam 1.3 (Beta).

- **Battle.net games are found by their product code** now, not only by name, so Warcraft III: Reforged (which Windows calls just "Warcraft III") shows as installed and plays.
- **The ▶ installed mark** now shows on Curator's picks, More like this and Your year too, not just the library.
- **Play and Install show the store's icon** ("Play on [Steam icon] Steam"), in the button and its menu.
- **More like this** moved to the very bottom of a game's page.
- **Notes are gone** from the game page. (Anything you'd written stays in your library file; it just isn't shown.)
- **Tighter game page:** shelves and the XGP / PS+ lines sit together right under the buttons, with space between "Included with XGP" and "Included with PS+".
- A game installed twice through the Xbox app no longer shows "Play on Xbox" twice.
