Test build 8 of Sunbeam 1.3 (Beta).

- **Fixed: XGP and PS+ games coming back as "unidentified" after a sync.** When an XGP / PS+ list name doesn't match your copy's name ("Tell Me Why: Chapters 1-3" on XGP, your Tell Me Why on Steam), matching it now sticks: Sunbeam remembers it, and later syncs keep it with your game. The ones that came back before this fix need matching one last time.
- **Updates that need a sync say so.** After installing a version whose new features need a sync to fill something in, a bar offers **Sync now** (or **Later**) once. An automatic sync that starts anyway takes care of it.
