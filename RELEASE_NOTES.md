Sunbeam 1.3: play your games from Sunbeam, keep your own shelves, and look back at your year.

- **Play.** Games installed through Steam, Epic, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net or the Xbox app get a **Play** button under their title (with the store's icon), which starts the game through its launcher. Installed in more than one place? The ▾ picks which. Games you have but haven't installed offer **Install** instead. Right-click › Play works too.
- **Installed games** have a small ▶ on their cover, everywhere covers show, and the Library has an **Installed** filter. Sunbeam reads what's installed from the launchers when it starts, after each sync and when you come back to its window.
- **Shelves:** lists you make, like "Couch co-op" or "Comfort games". Add a game from its page (**+ Add to shelf**) or with right-click › Add to shelf…; pick shelves in the Library's **Shelves** filter (rename or delete them there). **Your shelves** also show at the bottom of Curator, as cards or rows (Settings › Display).
- **Arrange Curator:** **Customize** (top right of Curator) reorders its sections and switches off the ones you don't need.
- **Stats**, a new tab: games played, games seen to the credits, your most played and the genres you played, for this year or earlier ones (‹ ›).
- **More like this** at the bottom of a game's page: similar games you own or can play on XGP / PS+.
- **Wishlist:** **My order** (Sort by) is your own ranking, set by dragging covers or with right-click › Move to top / bottom. A **section bar** stays at the top so Out now, In early access, Coming soon and No date yet are one click away. Free games show a **Free** tag.
- **Curator filter:** "Not marked" is now **Default**, between More and Less interested; **Sort › Curator mark** is new.
- **A new icon:** a sun rising over a controller.
- **Updates** are checked every time Sunbeam starts. An update that needs a sync to fill something in offers **Sync now** once.
- **Fixes:** XGP / PS+ games you matched to your own copy no longer come back as unidentified after a sync. The "couldn't be identified" count only counts games that sync couldn't place. The game page is tighter (the Notes section is gone). Battle.net games are found by their product code. Settings › Updates no longer wraps.
