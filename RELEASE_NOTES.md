Sunbeam 1.7: Nintendo Switch syncs by itself, and every sign-in is encrypted.

- **Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 sync like any other store.** Sign in to your Nintendo Account once (Settings › Nintendo Switch and Switch 2), and every sync reads the Play Activity the Nintendo Store app shows: every game you've played, exact play time and days played, first and last played, Switch or Switch 2, and each day's play (added to the game's Play history). No more screen recordings.
- Signing in: Sunbeam opens Nintendo's own sign-in page in your browser; right-click **Select this account**, copy the link and paste it into Sunbeam. Your password only goes to Nintendo. The sign-in lasts about two years.
- Games you imported from screen recordings become the synced copies (matched by name), so nothing is counted twice and their statuses, art and shelves stay. The screen-recording import is still there (Sync › Nintendo Switch, without signing in).
- **Every key, sign-in and account name is encrypted** in `settings.json`, with Windows' own data protection, tied to your Windows account on this PC. Ones saved by earlier versions are encrypted the first time this version starts. Copied to another PC or Windows user, sign-ins can't be read there and need doing again (Settings says which).
- **Ubisoft sign-in that sticks**: the Send to Sunbeam bookmark now asks Ubisoft for a "stay signed in" pass when the page has none (after Ubisoft's "Welcome back!" page, say), and the instructions explain that page.
- **Ubisoft's own tab**: the bookmark opens a Sunbeam tab that shows the import from start to finish ("51 Ubisoft games imported"), instead of turning your Ubisoft tab into Sunbeam. Your sign-in no longer passes through a web address, so it can't end up in your browser's history. Drag the bookmark again from Settings › Ubisoft Connect to get the new one.
