**Sunbeam 1.2: early access games**

- **Tagged:** games still in early access (from Steam, or IGDB for games not on Steam) get an "Early access" tag on their cover and game page, with the full release date once it's announced. Checked at every sync.
- **Wishlist:** early access games have their own **In early access** group (only shown when you have some). Once a full release date is announced it shows on the card, and the game appears in Curator's "Coming out soon"; when it fully releases it moves to Out now and "Recently released".
- **Never marked completed automatically** while in early access (earlier automatic marks on such games are undone), and their game page notes that time to beat and progress may still change.
- **New setting:** Settings › Early access games › "Hide early access games from the library and Curator".
- Long names now wrap onto a second line in Curator's Keep playing and Try something new.
