Sunbeam 1.4: GOG, and OpenCritic scores filling in faster.

- **GOG games in your library.** If GOG Galaxy is installed on this PC, Sunbeam reads your GOG games from it at every sync, with **hours played and last played**: nothing to set up, just tick GOG when you sync. Without Galaxy (or to add games Galaxy hasn't listed), sign in through GOG's sign-in link in Settings › GOG and paste the address you land on. GOG's website doesn't share hours played, so those only come from Galaxy.
- **Play and Install for GOG games.** Installed GOG games get the ▶ mark and a **Play** button, which starts them through GOG Galaxy (or directly, when Galaxy isn't installed). GOG games you haven't installed offer **Install**, which opens the game in Galaxy. GOG is in the first-run setup too.
- **Hidden automatically:** online betas, mod kits and editors (like The Witcher 3 REDkit), and expansion or season passes, like playtests and demos already were. You can still unhide any of them.
- **OpenCritic:** lookups no longer stop after the first one of each day (a pause from the day before carried over). Sunbeam now reads up to **180** scores a day, up from 140, and pauses for the day if OpenCritic reports that only 20 of the free plan's 200 are left.
- **Fixes:** the Sync window's step list named every store (it said "Import undefined library" for GOG).
