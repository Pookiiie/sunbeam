The first test build of Sunbeam 1.3 (Beta). Everything from 1.2.15, plus:

- **Play.** Games installed through Steam, Epic, Ubisoft Connect, Battle.net or the Xbox app get a **Play** button under their title, which starts the game through its launcher. Installed in more than one place? The ▾ picks which. Games you have but haven't installed offer **Install** instead (it opens the store's launcher). Right-click › Play works too.
- **Installed games** have a small ▶ on their cover, and the Library has an **Installed** filter. Sunbeam reads what's installed from the launchers when it starts, after each sync and when you come back to its window.
- **Shelves:** lists you make. Add a game from its page (**+ Add to shelf**) or right-click › Add to shelf…; pick shelves in the Library's new **Shelves** filter, where you can also rename or delete them.
- **More like this** on a game's page: games IGDB lists as similar that you own or can play on XGP / PS+. (Filled in in the background a little after Sunbeam starts.)
- **Your year**, a new tab: games played, games seen to the credits, your most played and the genres you played, for this year or earlier ones (‹ ›). A game counts as finished on the date of its finishing achievement, or the day you last played it.

Please tell me what doesn't work: Play and Install are new for every launcher, and I could only test them with stand-ins.
